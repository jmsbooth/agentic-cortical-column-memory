\subsection{Analysis commitments}

Three independent seeds and ten instances per task class are registered. Primary metrics are recall precision/recall, state reconstruction, temporal accuracy, relation accuracy, provenance accuracy, contradiction preservation, false-memory rate, stale-state rate, and relevant-context density. Report raw effects, 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals, paired effect sizes, and practical significance; apply Holm correction across confirmatory tests; retain timeouts and failures; perform no silent outlier removal or outcome-dependent stopping.

\subsection{Negative-results and version policy}

Registered results will be published whether hypotheses are supported, qualified, or rejected. A component that contributes nothing or increases resource cost will be reported. Corrections that do not alter registered elements may be v0.1.x releases with changelog entries. Changes to hypotheses, metrics, baselines, exclusions, or thresholds require a deviation record and a new protocol version. A completed empirical release requires all registered seeds, raw and processed data, generated tables and figures, matched-budget analyses, ablations, failure taxonomy, resource-quality Pareto analysis, and evidence-based abstract and conclusion updates.
