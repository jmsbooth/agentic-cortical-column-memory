\section{Conclusion}

CCM is proposed as a modular persistent-memory architecture for AI agents. Its central design proposition is that agent history can be maintained as multiple structured, reference-frame-relative models rather than reconstructed primarily through retained text or similarity retrieval. The architecture separates observations from derived state, preserves temporal history and provenance, supports bounded recall and context assembly, and reconciles partial models without requiring every recall to be a vote.

The \releaseversion{} release defines the architecture, reference implementation, invariants, deterministic validation tasks, memory-focused baselines, and preregistered protocol. Whether this organization improves memory quality, agent reasoning, or resource efficiency remains an empirical question. Small-model interaction and multi-agent composition are secondary extensions, and TPAA is neither required by nor part of the CCM definition.
